Notes on the table:

  • Qty 10 and 10k component prices come from the same Digi-Key / Newark pages (10-piece and reel breaks; where no 10k break exists the highest published break is used). See _build/unit_econ.py.
  • Certification: units 1 and 10 are pilot units under NRTL field evaluation (ASSUMPTION $5,000 per site and $1,500 per unit). From 100 up, a one-time listing program (ASSUMPTION $150,000, built from cited ranges in 03_Spec_Integration_Test_Cert/04_Certifications.md) plus one year of upkeep ($22,000, top of the cited ETL range) is spread over the first 1,000 units.
  • Freight is an ASSUMPTION pending carrier quotes. Warranty reserve is an ASSUMPTION of 2% of price; the cited 2025 average for US computer OEMs is 1.05% (Warranty Week, 16 July 2026).
  • Prices and royalties are PROPOSALS. Unit 1 is effectively break-even because a single hand-built, field-evaluated unit carries its own certification cost. Pilots use loaned units (see 06_Wedge_GTM/05_Pilot_Program_and_LOI.md).
  • Recurring revenue is separate: PROPOSED $150 per module per month for the witness, vault, and reports service.
