6. Integration patterns

  1. Lender / lessor: lender holds the authority role or a co-signing role on ARs; receives monthly evidence bundles and a signed collateral attestation (devices present, hours powered, energy, faults). Gate-open on default only per the credit agreement and applicable law.
  2. Operator billing: records feed metered billing per device and per tenant; customers verify their own bills with the SDK.
  3. OEM PDU: licensee embeds MQ-MAC-OEM, drives its own relays through the gate interface, exposes MonoQube endpoints next to its existing SNMP/Redfish.
  4. Regulated AI operator: records feed log retention (EU AI Act Article 12) as tamper-evident infrastructure logs; the record carries no model or data content.
