3. Conversion

If S1 to S6 pass and S7 is a yes, the customer may convert within 30 days to: (a) a fleet license (Model A or B) at the tier price, with pilot fees credited, and most-favored-licensee status; or (b) a 12-month subscription at the fleet rate. If any metric fails, Monotropic fixes and re-runs at no charge for 30 days, or the customer walks away with the final report.

4. First pilot targets

Three design-partner slots: one GPU operator with financed capacity, one metering OEM, and one lender or arranger. Names are not listed in this data room.
