MonoQube licensing

A controller in the power path to each compute bay. It reads the device identifier, powers the device only while a valid authorization is in force, meters the energy, and writes a signed, hash-chained operating record that an outside party can verify. It reads nothing from the workload.

Prepared 22 November 2026 for licensees. Figures are measured, cited, a design target, or proposed, as marked in each section. Confidential. Not an offer. Not legal advice.

License and patent

  • Per-unit royalty 6%, then 5%, then 4% of net price by volume, with a $40 floor. Or $0.002 per authorized device-hour, and a 20% share of recurring service.
  • Non-exclusive standard terms. One exclusive field is reserved for a lender partner.
  • Patent pending: PCT/US26/39996, filed 5 August 2026, claiming priority to 5 August 2025. Not yet published. National phase is about February 2028.

Pilot

Up to 10 feeders, 90 days, hardware on loan. Seven measured results, including energy within 0.5% of the site meter and acceptance of the monthly attestation. A successful pilot converts to a license, with fees credited.
