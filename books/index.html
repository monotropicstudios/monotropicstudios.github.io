MONOTROPIC

Books

  • Witness Me Teddy wakes late in a Los Angeles apartment measured by where the sunlight lands, a cat asleep across the uniform he must wear again. On the nightstand, a photograph of the man he misses still asks to be kissed.
  • Friends, Get Away Sophie arrives at a beach house grayer than its listing, a guitar over her shoulder and one suitcase for the weekend. She has almost cancelled the reunion three times, and the coffee left on the porch is still warm.
  • Texts From Grandma Gathered for Miriam, these are real messages from Grandma, the kind that find you exactly when you need them. An early one tries to send a new haircut and receives, in order, a ceiling fan, a thumb, and a short speech.
