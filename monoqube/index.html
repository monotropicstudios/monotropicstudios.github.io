Measure

Footprint
12 ft by 8 ft. Ground, rooftop, or marine deck. One vehicle. A level pad. Crane or forklift access.
Power
Three-phase. Voltage and amperage vary by SKU. UPS is supported.
Network
10GbE uplink minimum. 100GbE for GPU clusters. A separate out-of-band network.
Thermal
Self-contained cooling. Ambient range -20 C to 50 C.
Link
Dual-path isolation, LEO satellite fallback, and an independent control-plane power feed.

Three SKUs

MonoQube-C
Classical CPU. Control planes, scheduling, and inference. Also the control node for GPU and QPU systems.
MonoQube-G
GPU. Multi-GPU and mixed CPU plus GPU nodes for training and inference.
MonoQube-Q
Quantum interface. Classical control, FPGA timing, and cryogenic subsystems where the configuration needs them.
